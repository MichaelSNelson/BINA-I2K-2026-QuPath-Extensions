New Extensions for QuPath From simple (dialog manager, wizard wand, image export) to complex (DL cell and pixel classifiers, microscope control).

Tuesday 29 September 2026 · 10:30–12:30
Morgridge Hall, WARF Seminar Hub — Rm. 7560

QuPath is widely used for analysis of large 2D images, and its extension mechanism enables capabilities far beyond post-acquisition analysis. This workshop demonstrates a suite of open-source QuPath extensions developed at LOCI: microscope hardware control and automated acquisition, publication-quality export with integrated QUAREP-LiMi guidance, retrainable deep-learning pixel classification, slide-label OCR for automated metadata, enhanced annotation tools, classifier validation with bootstrap statistics, and polychromatic polarization microscopy.

Hour 1 simple → complex, with demos Hour 2 optional hands-on Requires QuPath 0.7.0+ 13 hands-on extensions

Sample data

Sara McArdle & Zbigniew Mikulski — La Jolla Institute for Immunology

The majority of the test and demonstration data used throughout this workshop was provided by Sara McArdle and Zbigniew Mikulski at LJI. Almost every exercise here — annotation, classifier training and validation, multiplexed analysis, export — was developed against their images. Tools like these cannot be built, tested, or taught without real data from people willing to share it. Sara McArdle also shaped the software directly: two of these extensions began as her Groovy scripts, and her FS2K course was the model for how these pages are structured.
